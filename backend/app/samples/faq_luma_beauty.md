# About Luma Beauty
Luma Beauty is an online store selling skincare and hair care products. This is a sample FAQ for the Inboxa demo.

Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm (Pacific Time). We reply to every email within one business day.

# Shipping
We ship to the United States, Canada and Mexico.

US orders arrive in 3–5 business days. International orders (Canada and Mexico) take 7–12 business days.

Shipping is free on US orders over $50. Orders under $50 pay a flat $5.99 shipping fee. International shipping is a flat $14.99.

Orders placed before 2pm Pacific Time ship the same business day. You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

# Order changes
You can change your delivery address or cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it, as long as it has not shipped. Reply to your order confirmation email with the change.

Once an order has shipped, we cannot change the address. You can contact the carrier using your tracking link.

# Returns and refunds
You can return unused items in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

Sale items can be returned within 14 days for store credit only.

To start a return, email us your order number. We will send you a free return label for US orders.

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the return.

# Damaged or wrong items
If your item arrives damaged or you received the wrong product, email us a photo within 7 days of delivery. We will send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. You do not need to return the damaged item.

# Billing
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay.

If you were charged twice, the duplicate charge is usually a temporary card hold that disappears within 3–5 business days. If it is still there after 5 days, email us your order number and we will refund it.

# Products
All our products are cruelty-free and vegan. They are made in the USA.

Our hair serum is safe for color-treated hair. Our face moisturizer is suitable for sensitive skin.

# Wholesale and partnerships
We work with salons, spas and boutiques. Wholesale orders start at 50 units per product. Our sales team will send a price list and set up a call. Please share your business name, location and expected monthly volume.
